Questions

Flying over, answered.

What am I flying over right now?

Open AirReveal and it uses your device's GPS to place you on the map and name the landmarks, cities, coastlines and natural wonders beneath and ahead of your plane, in real time.

Does AirReveal work in airplane mode?

Yes. Live tracking uses your device's GPS, which doesn't need in-flight Wi-Fi or a mobile signal. Download a flight's offline content before departure so maps and landmarks are ready for the journey.

Which side of the plane should I look from?

AirReveal tells you whether a landmark, or the sun at golden hour, is on the left or right of your aircraft, so you know which window to look out of.

Is AirReveal a flight-status app?

No. AirReveal is a personal flight companion for the journey itself. It is not an official flight-status, air-traffic or aviation-safety service, so follow your airline and airport for gates, delays and crew instructions.

Can I keep a flight journal and travel log?

Yes. Save every flight to your journal, collect the landmarks you discover, stamp your country passport and see your progress with achievements.

Do I need to know about planes to use it?

Not at all. Add where you're flying from and to, and AirReveal does the rest. Our user guide walks you through it and explains any terms and abbreviations you might see.

Is AirReveal free?

You can plan and preview flights and take your first Live-tracked flight for free. AirReveal Pro unlocks continued Live GPS tracking and the full in-flight experience.
