Two halves, one app

Look up. Look down.

Above you

Who's flying over?

A live radar of everything in the sky around you. Tap one to see its route, type and photo, or hold up your phone and the sky camera names each plane where it is.

Below you

The world beneath your wings.

From your own seat: the landmarks below, which side to look from, and a journal of every flight. Works offline, at 38,000 feet.

New in 2.0

Blue lights, spotted.

Police, air ambulance, coastguard, firefighting and military aircraft stand out in their own service's colours, like the UK's black-and-yellow police helicopters. Turn on alerts and AirReveal tells you when one comes near, even with the app closed.
