Frequently asked questions

What's free, and what needs AirReveal Pro?

Planning a flight — creating a route, browsing it on the map, discovering landmarks, downloading offline content — is completely free with no limits. Live-tracking a real flight with your device's GPS, including offline map support while you're tracking, requires AirReveal Pro after your first free Live flight.

How do I subscribe or cancel AirReveal Pro?

You'll be offered AirReveal Pro the moment you try to start or resume Live tracking beyond your first free flight, or any time from the Profile page. To cancel, go to your device's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → AirReveal, or tap Manage Subscription on AirReveal's own Profile page. Cancelling stops future renewals but doesn't refund the current period; you keep Pro access until it ends.

I reinstalled the app or got a new device — how do I get my subscription back?

Open AirReveal, go to Profile, and tap Restore Purchases. This restores an active subscription or Lifetime purchase tied to your Apple ID without charging you again.

A flight I was tracking live disappeared, or Pro stopped working mid-flight.

This shouldn't happen — a flight you're already Live-tracking when a subscription lapses keeps tracking uninterrupted. The subscription check only applies when you start or resume tracking a new flight. If you're seeing something different, please email us with details.

Where is my data stored? Is it backed up?

Everything you enter stays on your device by default — AirReveal has no servers of its own. If you tap Sync Now on the Profile page, your data also syncs to your own private iCloud account, which both backs it up and keeps it current across your devices. Deleting the app removes its local data; if you'd synced to iCloud, that copy remains in your iCloud account.

How do I export or back up my flights and journal?

Go to Profile → Export All Trip Data for a full JSON export of every flight and journal entry, or use a single flight's own Export action in My Flights. Use Import Trip Data to bring a previous export back in. All of this happens entirely on your device.

Why does AirReveal need my location?

Only for Live tracking — plotting your real GPS position against your planned route and detecting landmarks nearby. Location is never requested while you're just planning a flight. You can review or revoke it anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → AirReveal.

Can I trust AirReveal for real flight status, gate changes, or delays?

No — AirReveal isn't connected to any airline or air-traffic-control system. It's a personal planning and GPS-tracking companion, not an official source of flight status. Always check your airline's own app or airport displays for that. See our Terms of Use for details.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

Email info@jaywales.com with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help a lot.
