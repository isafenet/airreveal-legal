2 What AirReveal is

AirReveal is a personal flight-companion app. It helps you plan a flight's route, track your own real flight live using your device's GPS, and discover landmarks, countries, and points of interest along the way — offline, wherever possible. It also keeps a personal journal of your completed trips. It is for your personal, non-commercial use.

Planning a flight — creating a route, previewing it on the map, browsing landmarks, downloading offline content — is always free, and so is seeing the planes in the sky around you, updated about once a minute. Live tracking a real flight with your device's GPS, including offline map support while you're tracking, requires an AirReveal Pro subscription (your first Live-tracked flight is free — see Section 5). So does Live Sky: planes updated every few seconds, Sit in Its Cockpit, Watch It Fly and Look Around Here at any airport.

3 Not an official flight-tracking or aviation-safety tool

AirReveal is not connected to any airline, airport, or air-traffic-control system, and it does not receive live flight-status data from any aviation authority. Route generation, flight numbers, and schedules you enter or import are for your own planning and record-keeping only.

Never rely on AirReveal for real-time flight status, gate information, delays, cancellations, navigation, or any safety-critical decision. Always use your airline's own app, airport displays, or staff for that information. AirReveal's Live tracking reflects your device's own GPS position — it is not a substitute for official flight information.

Routes, positions, landmarks, weather and the planes shown around you may be incomplete, delayed or inaccurate. This section explains those limits so you can make informed choices. It does not remove our responsibility where we have been negligent (see Section 12).

Live tracking uses your device's GPS, including in the background while the app isn't open, so your route and progress keep updating over the course of a real flight.
