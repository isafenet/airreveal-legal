By downloading, installing, or using AirReveal, you agree to these Terms of Use. If you don't agree, please don't use the app. These terms apply alongside AirReveal's Privacy Policy, which describes how your data is handled.

2 What AirReveal is

AirReveal is a personal flight-companion app. It helps you plan a flight's route, track your own real flight live using your device's GPS, and discover landmarks, countries, and points of interest along the way — offline, wherever possible. It also keeps a personal journal of your completed trips.

Planning a flight — creating a route, previewing it on the map, browsing landmarks, downloading offline content — is always free. Live tracking a real flight with your device's GPS, including offline map support while you're tracking, requires an AirReveal Pro subscription (your first Live-tracked flight is free — see Section 5).

3 Not an official flight-tracking or aviation-safety tool

AirReveal is not connected to any airline, airport, or air-traffic-control system, and it does not receive live flight-status data from any aviation authority. Route generation, flight numbers, and schedules you enter or import are for your own planning and record-keeping only.

Never rely on AirReveal for real-time flight status, gate information, delays, cancellations, or any safety-critical decision. Always use your airline's own app, airport displays, or staff for that information. AirReveal's Live tracking reflects your device's own GPS position — it is not a substitute for official flight information.

7 Location & offline maps

Live tracking uses your device's GPS, including in the background while the app isn't open, so your route and progress keep updating over the course of a real flight. You can revoke location permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → AirReveal, though doing so will stop Live tracking from working. Offline map and content downloads use publicly available map tiles and landmark data (see Section 10) and don't require any location permission beyond what Live tracking itself needs.
